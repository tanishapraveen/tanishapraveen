## Featured project

### CurrentZero

A cybersecurity education and analysis platform designed to help people identify and understand phishing threats in suspicious URLs, QR codes, emails, and SMS messages.

- Combines passive analysis with structured evidence, understandable explanations, and interactive learning activities.
- Separates risk from confidence and communicates uncertainty when checks are unavailable.
- Uses development-stage detection methods; empirical validation of machine-learning classifiers is ongoing.

[Explore CurrentZero](https://currentzero.144-24-18-73.sslip.io/)

## Research in progress

### TACTIC-TRACE — Phishing-detection robustness

Investigating how phishing-detection approaches respond to evolving and modified attacks.

- Designing controlled comparisons of URL, visual, and DOM-based indicators across legitimate and phishing webpages.
- Developing a taxonomy of webpage modifications and Python/Playwright workflows for reproducible feature extraction and evaluation.
- Exploring detection reliability, false positives, and performance under changing attack conditions. Research is ongoing.

### DigitalLife — Ecological early warning and adaptive measurement

Exploring whether machine learning can detect approaching collapse and select useful measurement times under a limited observation budget in simulated ecological systems. The experimental framework includes an ExtraTrees warning model, fitted Q-learning scheduling, baseline comparisons, and false-alarm calibration. Research is ongoing.

## Other projects

### Student Lens: Youth Pulse

An interactive civic technology prototype exploring ways student perspectives could inform local government decisions. Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, it demonstrates topic-based surveys, an idea board, and a staff reporting preview using fictional sample data. Currently a proposed pilot, not an official city service.
